Overhear

Live captions over anything on your phone.

Download for Android All downloads

Works on almost every phone. If yours is an emulator or an older Intel tablet, use All downloads and pick the x86_64 build.

After downloading

  • Tap the file to install. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser; this is normal for an app that is not from the Play Store.
  • Open Overhear and press Start captions.
  • It asks for three permissions in order, each explained on screen.

The speech model (about 42 MB) downloads the first time you press Start. Everything runs on your phone; no audio is uploaded.
